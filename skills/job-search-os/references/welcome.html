    Step 1 · Welcome

    Job searching should not eat your whole day.

    Job Search Harness helps you find verified roles, prepare truthful resume drafts, and learn from results. This short guide takes you through setup and your first run, one step at a time.

    Your personal files stay in a separate private workspace. You make every final decision.

    What you will do

    1. Understand how the harness works
    2. Install it in a file-capable AI agent
    3. Give it a starting direction
    4. Run one search and review the results
    Step 2 · How it works

    One connected loop.

    The harness remembers your direction and carries the repeatable work between sessions.

    01 · AIM

    Set your direction

    Record the roles, locations, constraints, and strengths that matter to you.

    02 · FIND

    Check real openings

    Search for suitable jobs and verify each promising role on the employer’s application page.

    03 · PREPARE

    Review a truthful draft

    See the strongest matches and resume drafts based only on your confirmed experience.

    04 · LEARN

    Use the outcome

    Record replies and interviews so future searches reflect what you have learned.

    Step 3 · Your control

    You stay in charge.

    The harness prepares and tracks work. You decide what is accurate, worthwhile, and ready to send.

    You decide

    • Which jobs deserve your time
    • Whether facts and numbers are accurate
    • Whether a resume is ready
    • When and where to apply

    The harness handles

    • Finding and checking openings
    • Explaining fit and gaps
    • Preparing drafts for review
    • Keeping a record of outcomes
    Privacy boundary: keep your resume, profile, tracker, and search preferences in a separate private folder. The public toolkit and its read-only connector do not store your personal files.
    Step 4 · Get ready

    Use an AI agent that can work with files.

    The quick test: can it open a folder on your computer, read files, and create new ones? If so, it can probably run the full harness.

    Direct install

    ChatGPT Codex

    Use Codex on your computer to install the toolkit and create a private workspace.

    Open a folder

    Claude Cowork or Code

    Give the agent access to the downloaded toolkit folder.

    Open a folder

    Cursor or VS Code agent

    Use the AI agent in the editor with permission to read and edit local files.

    Using ChatGPT or Claude in a normal chat? The read-only connector can teach the public method. A full run still needs a file-capable agent and your private workspace.

    Step 10 · First manual run
    Find a small, verified shortlist.
    Start manually. Your agent should check the employer’s application page, explain fit and concerns, and update your primary tracker.
    
        
    Find today’s roles
    Run my Job Search Harness for today. Read my current goals and recent feedback first. Find and verify suitable roles, update my primary tracker, and give me a short digest. Stop after the digest. Do not create resumes or apply to anything.
    
        
    Read the shortlist before continuing. Open each employer page yourself if a role interests you. The harness should flag uncertainty rather than guess.
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Step 11 · Prepare and check
    Draft, then review.
    For promising roles, ask for a truthful resume draft and a separate quality check. You still make the final call.
    
        
    
          
    Prepare resume drafts
    Run after the search finishes.
    Run my separate resume-tailoring pass. Use the scores and verified roles in my primary tracker. Create only eligible drafts, use only facts I have confirmed, and save every draft as Needs Human Review. Do not apply or create cover letters.
    
          
    Check the drafts
    Run after resume tailoring finishes.
    Run my separate resume-QA pass. Check the latest drafts against my verified facts and resume rules, inspect every PDF page, record the verdicts in my primary tracker, and leave every draft as Needs Human Review.
    
        
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Step 12 · Keep learning
    Tell the harness what happened.
    Record replies, rejections, and interviews. Your agent can suggest changes based on repeated evidence. Test the process manually before scheduling it.
    
        
    Share an outcome
    Update my Job Search Harness with this outcome: [TELL IT WHAT HAPPENED]. Tell me what this changes—and what it does not change yet.
    
        
    Can the harness apply for me?
    It prepares shortlists and drafts. You review everything and choose when to apply.
    
        
    Will it guarantee I pass an ATS?
    No harness can promise that. It helps match truthful experience to the language of a role.
    
        
    How do I update an existing installation?
    Refresh the public toolkit, then compare its reusable rules with your private workspace. Keep your profile, tracker, and search settings. See the update instructions.
