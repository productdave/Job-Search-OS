## Fit-scoring rubric

Score each category, explain the evidence, and total to 10.

| Dimension | Points | Guidance |
|---|---:|---|
| Role and scope fit | 0–3 | Seniority, ownership, and day-to-day work |
| Evidence match | 0–3 | Candidate has verified achievements that answer the role |
| Domain and problem fit | 0–2 | Preferred domain or transferable problem |
| Logistics and constraints | 0–2 | Location, compensation, authorization, and exclusions |

Use the canonical eligibility threshold from `run-config.md`. A high score never overrides a hard exclusion or an unverified listing.
