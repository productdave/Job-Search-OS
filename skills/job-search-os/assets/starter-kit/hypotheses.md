# Hypotheses

These are ideas being tested, not facts. Use stable IDs and preserve counter-evidence.

## Status rules

- `Forming`: insufficient evidence.
- `Confirmed`: meets the evidence rule in `run-config.md`.
- `Disproved`: evidence consistently contradicts the claim.
- `Merged`: incorporated into another stable ID.
- `Superseded`: replaced by a newer formulation.
