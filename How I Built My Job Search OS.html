How the Job Search OS works

A practical blueprint for separating personal facts, search decisions, automation logic, resume production, and learning history so the system improves without becoming impossible to maintain.

The design principle

The live Markdown workspace owns the workflow. Agents and schedulers read it; they do not keep separate copies of it.

Change process once. Preferences go in search-config.md, runtime thresholds in run-config.md, and durable steps in the relevant runbook. Scheduled prompts stay as thin pointers.

Candidate layer

profile.md and resume-style-guide.md contain verified facts and presentation rules.

Decision layer

search-config.md, hypotheses.md, and search-patterns.md explain what to pursue and why.

Operations layer

run-config.md and the runbooks define thresholds, caps, integrations, read order, and hard stops.

Evidence layer

The role tracker, keyword ledger, and learning log preserve outcomes and make future decisions auditable.

The daily loop

Verification comes before scoring. Scoring comes before resume work. Human action remains outside the automated run.

Resume production

A separate resume pass reads only eligible, live roles. It selects and reframes verified achievements from profile.md, generates a draft, renders it, and leaves it marked for human review.

Truth constraint

No metric, employer, responsibility, title, date, or public URL can be invented.

Idempotency

A valid existing draft is skipped unless regeneration is explicitly requested.

Visual QA

Generated pages are rendered and checked for clipping, spacing, broken links, and formatting drift.

Human gate

Automated output remains a draft. Applying and sending outreach are separate human decisions.

Learning without context bloat

The system keeps current rules small while preserving exact history behind indexes and archives.
