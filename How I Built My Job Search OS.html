How the Job Search Harness works

A practical blueprint for wrapping an AI agent in verified facts, search rules, safety boundaries, resume standards, and feedback—so repeated work gets easier without handing over the final decision.

The harness principle

A model can produce an answer. The harness turns that ability into a repeatable process. The live Markdown workspace owns the workflow; agents and schedulers read it instead of keeping separate copies.

Change process once. Preferences go in search-config.md, runtime thresholds in run-config.md, and durable steps in the relevant runbook. Scheduled prompts stay as thin pointers.

Candidate layer

profile.md and resume-style-guide.md contain verified facts and presentation rules.

Decision layer

search-config.md, hypotheses.md, and search-patterns.md explain what to pursue and why.

Operations layer

run-config.md and the runbooks define thresholds, caps, integrations, read order, and hard stops.

Evidence layer

The role tracker, keyword ledger, and learning log preserve outcomes and make future decisions auditable.

The daily loop

Verification comes before scoring. Scoring comes before resume work. Human action remains outside the automated run.

Resume production

Two ordered passes follow the search. Resume tailoring reads only eligible, verified roles and creates drafts. Resume QA then checks the exact drafts from that run. Both use the same primary tracker as their handoff.

Truth constraint

No metric, employer, responsibility, title, date, or public URL can be invented.

Eligibility and idempotency

The score rule comes from run-config.md. The listing must be live in the preceding search run, and a valid existing draft is skipped unless regeneration was requested.

Separate visual QA

The QA pass renders every generated page, checks facts, links, clipping, spacing, and formatting, and records its verdict without approving submission.

Human gate

Automated output remains a draft. Applying and sending outreach are separate human decisions.

Learning without context bloat

The harness keeps current rules small while preserving exact history behind indexes and archives.
